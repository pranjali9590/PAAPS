# 🎮 Play_time - The Official PAAPS Bunk Experience ✨

A delightful, highly-animated interactive game created with pastel lavender & retro arcade aesthetics.

## 🚀 How to Run

You have two instant options:

### Option 1: Direct Double Click (No setup needed)
Double-click `index.html` to open it in any web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).

### Option 2: 1-Click Flask Server
Double-click `run.bat` (or run `python app.py` in your terminal). It will automatically launch the Flask local server and open your browser at `http://127.0.0.1:5000`.

## 🎨 Features & Flow

1. **Screen 1: The Attend Trap 😏**
   - Runaway "Yes" button with physics evasion zone (teleports/glides away when your mouse/finger gets within 40px, complete with witty speech bubbles and spring sound effects).
   - Glowing pastel green "No" button that triggers a camera-shutter snapshot transition.

2. **Screen 2: The Bunk Pitch 🐱**
   - Features the adorable innocent kitten accomplice peeking from a cozy lavender blanket.
   - Celebratory pink pulsating "Yes" button triggering confetti and the "Welcome to the Club" pop-up.

3. **Screen 3: PAAPS Club Interview – Question 1 🩺**
   - 3D interactive alibi card slider (Fainting, Secret Mission, Aggressive Coughing).

4. **Screen 4: PAAPS Club Interview – Question 2 🍫**
   - Essential bunking supplies (Snacks, Moral Support + Hotspot, High-voltage Gossip).

5. **Screen 5: PAAPS Club Interview – Question 3 🤝**
   - Victory celebration (Overly complicated handshake, cheap iced coffees, 4-hour nap).

6. **Screen 6: The Ultimate Campus Loyalty Test 👑**
   - **Events comm (The Only Best Club!)**: Crowned champion card with golden sparkle.
   - **BPR & Other clubs**: Evasive buzzer error! Clicking them playfully rejects the selection and urges you to pick Events comm.

7. **Screen 7: Official PAAPS Member Card Loaded 🎊**
   - 3D tilted holographic digital receipt card.
   - Real-time shimmering glitter particle system.
   - Custom assigned role based on your choices (e.g. *Dramatic Faint & Snack Smuggler*).
   - "Events comm Approved" and "Certified Bunker" official stamps.
   - SVG barcode and Member ID.
   - **Download Pass button**: Renders and downloads your customized high-res PAAPS Member Pass card PNG directly!

8. **Web Audio Sound Effects 🔊**
   - Built-in zero-latency synthesized sound effects for pops, boings, camera shutters, party fanfares, and buzzers. Mute toggle included in top HUD.
